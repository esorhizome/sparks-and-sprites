# 08 · Free & legal sources

*Fresh-start note: none needed — this chapter runs on plain English plus the license names it defines itself, below.*

---

Everything here is legitimate to use — but licenses differ in what they ask of you.

## The 60-second license glossary

- **CC0** — do anything, commercial included, credit not required (crediting is still a kind gesture).
- **CC-BY** — do anything, but credit the author wherever your project lists credits.
- **CC-BY-SA** — credit, and anything you build *from the asset itself* must carry the same license.
- **CC-BY-NC** — non-commercial only. **This is Shadertoy's default** — treat Shadertoy as a place to learn how effects work, not to copy from, unless a specific shader states otherwise.
- **MIT / OFL** — very permissive code / font licenses; keep the license text in your credits.

Full legal texts: [creativecommons.org/licenses](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/). Rule of thumb: **the license is per-asset, not per-site** — always glance at the individual item's page.

## One honest caution

"Found it on Google Images / Pinterest / an AI mood-board" is not a license. When in doubt, the sites above make doubt unnecessary — that's exactly why this table exists.
